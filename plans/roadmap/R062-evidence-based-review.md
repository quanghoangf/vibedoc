# R062: Evidence-based review
**Parent:** R003
**Status:** in-progress
**Order:** 100
**Tasks:** T163, T164, T165, T166

Review becomes "look at the proof and decide": the human approves or sends back from the evidence instead of re-testing by hand.

**In scope:** the Review column and task panel open the evidence doc directly, approve → done, send back points at the failing or doubtful step (with its screenshot) so the agent knows exactly what to fix, review state per item
**Out of scope:** multi-reviewer approval, review assignment
**Done when:** a task in review can be approved or sent back from its evidence in one place, and the send-back note names the step
