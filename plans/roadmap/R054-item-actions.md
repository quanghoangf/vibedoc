# R054: Item actions
**Parent:** R002
**Status:** planned
**Order:** 150
**Tasks:** T074, T075, T076, T077, T078, T079

Every common action on a task, epic or doc can be found and done right where the item is. Today deleting an epic means opening its Edit form, tasks can't be edited or deleted from the UI at all, and doc actions only show up when you hover over the doc list.

**In scope:** a ⋯ / right-click menu on roadmap nodes, task cards and docs; editing and deleting tasks; Undo after a delete; bulk status change and delete; keyboard shortcuts and command-palette entries for the selected item
**Out of scope:** new properties (R055); undoing edits other than deletes
**Done when:** a user can delete, duplicate or move a task, an epic or a doc in two clicks or one shortcut, and undo the delete
