  No generic kanban tool has these.
- **Category-interchangeable:**
  - The roadmap mind map (roadmap.sh) with its default camera.

## Overall Impression

VibeDoc has a real voice and a few excellent moments. Test review is the best page, and the docs landing with its agent usage data is the second. But the plan → supervise → review loop, which is the reason the product exists, is undermined by numbers you can't trust: a progress bar that reads done when nothing is done, four "needs you" counters, and an Approve with no undo. The biggest opportunity: **make "what's running, what waits on me" one designed surface with one count, and make every page's first viewport answer that before showing history.**

## What's Working

1. **Test review (/manual-tests):**
   - The triage headline sentence and j/k.
   - The replay, which auto-pauses with the caption "Step 2 · Click the button → it reads Clicked".
   - Expected/Received sitting next to "Send back to agent…".
   - Its mobile layout holds.

   It is the emotional peak of the whole app.
2. **Pages that explain the agent's logic to the human:**
   - The By epic view, with "also after T181", "Waiting on T198" and the note about the `vibedoc_next_task` order.
   - The Docs landing, with "Never read by agents" and "Searched, not found: Electron".
   - Activity sessions, with "Connected, nothing changed".

   No other tool has these.
3. **The keyboard and help system:**
   - Skip links ("Skip to agent status").
   - A 2px focus ring.
   - `?` opens the Help panel as a region, not a trap.
   - ⌘K lists chats first.
   - The graph has real keyboard navigation and edges with aria-labels.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Review looks like done, and progress contradicts itself**
- **Why it matters:** the R109 epic sheet says "0% · 0 of 4 tasks done" next to a fully filled bar, because review segments fill with the solid accent. With a green accent, Review (accent) and Done (teal) are almost the same color on bars, chips and the context rail. The sidebar draws Review amber. This breaks trust in the product's core promise.
- **Fix:**
  - Draw review segments hatched or outlined, never solid.
  - Change the label to "0% done · 4 in review".
  - Give Review its own status hue that doesn't depend on the accent, the same everywhere (sidebar, board, roadmap, activity).
  - The accent is only for selection (the Highlighter Rule).
- **Command:** /impeccable colorize (status palette) + /impeccable clarify (progress copy)

**[P1] One "needs you" queue, not four counters; the sidebar mirrors the pages**
- **Why it matters:**
  - Sidebar Board 20, Manual tests 24, Inbox 24 and the Review column 19 never say whether they are the same thing.
  - The sidebar's page children push Docs/Memory/Graph below the fold at 1440×900, with no scroll cue.
  - The sidebar has no `<nav>` landmark.
  - /chat shows two chat lists at once.
- **Fix:**
  - One `needsYou` definition and one badge, on Inbox.
  - The sidebar becomes 8 flat rows with badges, page children collapsed (at most 1–3 "needs you" items), no "Viewed".
  - Wrap it in `<nav aria-label="Main">` and add an overflow fade.
  - On /chat, collapse the sidebar Chats group.
- **Command:** /impeccable distill (shell) + /impeccable layout

**[P1] The end of the loop (Approve) has no safety and no closure**
- **Why it matters:** `inbox/page.tsx:72` `decide()` removes the row and calls a plain `toast()`, with no undo. The row shows no evidence (checks proven, last run). Rows are newest-first, so the only failed run sits at the bottom under 20 identical Approve/Send back pairs. There are no keys. After approving, nothing tells you "epic now 3/4".
- **Fix:**
  - Use `undoToast` for both actions.
  - Show "1/7 checks · no run" in the row.
  - Make Approve the quiet style when checks remain.
  - Sort failed runs and auto-fix-limit items first.
  - Add a/s/j/k.
  - Add a closure line after approving.
- **Command:** /impeccable harden + /impeccable delight (closure)

**[P1] Default views open on history; wide diagrams are clipped**
- **Why it matters:**
  - /roadmap opens its camera on the finished "Shipped" horizon, and Fit at 105 nodes is confetti.
  - /board's default swimlanes put paused or done lanes first and push the Review cards below the fold.
  - The roadmap Timeline is empty on real data, and the TODAY line cuts across its empty message.
  - In /docs, HLD.md's sequence diagram is cut off on the right at 1440 px. The scrollbar is a 4px hairline at the bottom of an 800px diagram.
- **Fix:**
  - The roadmap opens on the first horizon that has active epics, with shipped horizons collapsed into one node.
  - Board lanes sort review and in-progress first.
  - The timeline falls back to Started/Done when there is no due date.
  - Diagrams can break out of the prose measure, with an edge fade plus a "Fit / Scroll →" chip.
- **Command:** /impeccable layout + /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Dead, duplicated or off-system surfaces**
- **Why it matters:**
  - The Settings → Skills and Agents tabs: `DEFAULT_SKILLS`/`DEFAULT_AGENTS` are read only by `api/settings` and the settings page, and nothing uses them. They show emoji and placeholder personas, so they are UI that pretends to give control.
  - /setup and /welcome don't use `useT()` (a violation of R078), and /welcome says 39 tools.
  - /explorer duplicates /docs. Its descriptions leak `<!-- BEGIN… -->` and frontmatter, and it breaks at 390 px.
  - There are 3 connect commands, and the menu says "Settings → MCP", a tab that doesn't exist.
- **Fix:**
  - Delete Skills/Agents, leaving 6 settings tabs.
  - Merge /setup into /start, and restyle /setup as a ruled form, in i18n.
  - Fold explorer descriptions into a column in /docs, and drop the Treemap.
  - Make one recommended connect path (user scope) the only primary.
- **Command:** /impeccable distill + /impeccable onboard

## Minor Observations

- **Status names:** "start"/"pause" are lowercase next to Title Case buttons in the task panel. The status is "ACTIVE" on the roadmap but "In progress" on the board. The H1 says "Test review" while the nav says "Manual tests".
- **Emoji:** emoji in the UI (❌ in the Evidence heading, 🤖 in "No run yet") go against DESIGN.md. "2 steps not in the checklist" is drawn in the accent, as if it were good news.
- **Board:** the Table view starves the Title column (about 12 characters). The Timeline bars are all the same length to "now". The dashed Done rail looks like a drop target.
- **Chat:** the list doesn't dedupe (three "Verify task T196" rows), and titles are cut from the raw prompt. The context rail's Brief loses its markdown structure. The help button covers the send button at 390 px.
- **Docs:** the Linked docs rail is open by default with an unreadable 24-node diagram. The Outline popover covers that rail. The lint strip pushes search down 300 px.
- **Memory:** the handoff H1 is larger than the page title. The handoff is 23 days stale with no age badge. Cleanup only has Dismiss, and zero-count type chips still show.
- **Activity:** each row says the same thing three times (icon + chip + sentence). There are 18 ID-only chips. "0/2" sits next to a full bar.
- **Graph:** "Project memory" and "Project Memory" can't be told apart. The Unlinked shelf covers the bottom of the graph. Search doesn't frame the matches.
- **Mobile:** the breadcrumb truncates the project name to "v…" or blank. The roadmap toolbar wraps onto 3 rows. Inbox titles are cut to 2 words. The settings tabs scroll sideways with no cue.
- **DESIGN.md:** the frontmatter and the prose section disagree on the type ramp (1.1 vs 1.25rem). Settings → Appearance shows a blue swatch while DESIGN says violet.

## Questions to Consider

- If supervising counts as much as planning, why do the board and roadmap open on history? Should every page's first viewport answer "what's running, what waits on me"?
- Should Inbox be the only "needs you" place, with the Review column, the "Needs you" tab and the sidebar as filters of one queue?
- With 10 settings tabs, /setup, /welcome and /explorer: which would you miss if they disappeared tomorrow?
- Should the empty board *be* the welcome screen, instead of a separate /start with 7 asks?
